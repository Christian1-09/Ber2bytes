Put your images in this folder (same file names), then rebuild the app:

  logo.png         Your Ber2Bytes logo for WHITE backgrounds (black "COMPUTER PARTS" text).
                   Transparent PNG works best, about 600px wide. Shown on the right side of the login.
  logo-dark.png    (optional) Logo version for BLACK backgrounds (white text).
                   Shown in the sidebar and on the left side of the login. If missing, logo.png is used.
                   If no logo files exist, a text version of the logo is shown.

  login-hero.jpg   The picture on the left side of the login screen
                   (PC parts / gaming setup). About 1200 x 1400 px.
                   If this file is missing, a dark red/black background is shown.

  app.ico          (optional) Window/taskbar icon. Then uncomment <ApplicationIcon> in Ber2Bytes.csproj.
